FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Answers for Mission Operators

Everything you need to know about deployment, data latency, and privacy.

What hardware or software do I need to run RJ God's Eye™? expand_more
None. RJ God's Eye™ runs directly inside any modern web browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox) on desktop, tablet, or mobile. It is accelerated by WebGL 2.0 and hosted on Cloudflare's global edge network for sub-second load times.
How fresh is the flight and vessel tracking data? expand_more
Aircraft ADS-B feeds refresh every 5 to 10 seconds via OpenSky Network and adsb.lol. Maritime AIS vessel feeds stream continuously over real-time WebSockets via AISStream, ensuring true sub-minute vessel positional accuracy.
Can I ingest my company's private security cameras or vehicle GPS feeds? expand_more
Yes. Under the Enterprise Sovereign plan, RJ Business Solutions provides custom RTSP, HLS, and WebRTC video ingestion pipelines to map your private facility cameras, drone fleets, and GPS trackers directly onto the 3D globe.
Are my searches and tracking coordinates private? expand_more
Yes. All telemetry queries are encrypted in transit via TLS 1.3. We do not sell or log customer coordinate surveillance data to third parties.
