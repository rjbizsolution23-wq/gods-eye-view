# Executive Summary
The global Geospatial Analytics and Open Source Intelligence (OSINT) market is expanding rapidly, driven by corporate security teams, risk analysts, defense contractors, maritime/aviation logistics operators, investigative journalists, and high-net-worth family offices requiring unified situational awareness.

---

# Target Customer Segments (ICPs)
1. **Corporate Security & Executive Protection Teams:** Need live situational monitoring of international assets, flight paths, weather, and civil incidents.
2. **Maritime & Aviation Logistics Operators:** Require real-time vessel/fleet tracking combined with seismic, weather, and port congestion telemetry.
3. **OSINT Analysts & Risk Consultants:** Professional investigators seeking 3D spatial intelligence overlays without managing dozens of fragmented tabs.
4. **Fintech & Commodity Traders:** Monitoring real-time supply chain bottlenecks, tanker movements, and critical infrastructure status.

---

# Value Gap & Unmet Market Demand
Current enterprise tools (Palantir, Planet Labs, Maxar) cost $50,000–$250,000+/year and require complex procurement. Consumer flight/vessel trackers (Flightradar24, MarineTraffic) are siloed single-purpose tools. 

**The Opportunity:** A unified, photorealistic 3D Spatial Intelligence Command Center with voice AI control and multi-domain feed fusion, accessible at accessible SaaS price points ($49–$499/mo).
