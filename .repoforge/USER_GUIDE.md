## 🏛 1. System Overview
**RJ God's Eye™** is a real-time, 3D autonomous spatial intelligence and planetary operations platform. It aggregates and renders live multi-domain feeds (Air, Sea, Space, Ground, CCTV, Environmental Hazards) on a photorealistic 3D digital twin of planet Earth.

## 🕹️ 2. Core Navigation & 3D Camera Controls

### Mouse & Trackpad Inputs
- **Pan Surface:** Left Click + Drag across the planet.
- **Smooth Zoom:** Scroll Wheel or Right Click + Drag up/down.
- **Pitch & Tilt Angle:** Middle Click + Drag or `Ctrl` + Left Click Drag.
- **Heading Rotation:** `Shift` + Left Click Drag.
- **Entity Inspection:** Double-click on any flying aircraft or maritime vessel to lock target.

### Essential Keyboard Hotkeys
- `R`: Instant Horizon Leveling & Camera Reset.
- `Space`: Toggle Tactical Cockpit First-Person HUD view.
- `F`: Enter/Exit True Fullscreen Mission Mode.
- `1` to `5`: Quick-switch between active telemetry layer presets.
- `Esc`: Dismiss active modals, dialogs, or deselect targets.

## 📡 3. Live Telemetry Data Layers

### ✈️ Aviation Telemetry (ADS-B)
- Live commercial, cargo, and military flights streamed via global receiver networks.
- Real-time altitude, calibrated airspeed, squawk identification, and flight route vectors.

### 🚢 Maritime Telemetry (AIS)
- Container cargo ships, crude oil tankers, passenger liners, and tugs.
- Real-time vessel draft, speed over ground, heading, and destination port data.

### 🛰️ Orbital Satellites (SGP4 Propagation)
- CelesTrak TLE orbital propagation for Starlink constellations, GPS satellites, and scientific orbiters.
- Real-time orbital inclination tracks and dynamic ground footprints.

### 📹 Ground CCTV & Environmental Hazards
- Live streaming municipal traffic camera networks with direct video player modals.
- USGS real-time seismic earthquake pins (scaled by Richter magnitude).
- NASA FIRMS active thermal wildfire hotspot overlays.

## 🛡️ 6. Troubleshooting & Customer Support

### Common Questions
1. **How do I activate live CCTV feeds?**  
   Open the bottom control dock, toggle the **CCTV & Hazards** layer, and zoom into any supported metropolitan area (e.g., San Francisco, Austin, London, Tokyo). Click any camera marker to launch the live video feed.
2. **How do I upgrade or manage my billing?**  
   Click the **⚡ UPGRADE PLAN** button in the top HUD bar to access the self-serve Stripe billing portal.
